## The core problem statistic (verified)

Source: [American Mosque Survey 2020, ISPU](https://ispu.org/reports-and-analysis/report-1-mosque-survey-2020/)

- "The vast majority of mosques (76%) are managed entirely by volunteers."
- 2,769 mosques identified in the U.S. in 2020 — a 31% increase from 2,106 in 2010.
- Average mosque annual operating budget: **$276,500**. Median: **$80,000**.
- These are large enough organizations to have real operational complexity, but small enough
  that almost none of them can afford a paid operations/admin staff — that gap is the whole
  thesis. Don't round this up into "mosques have $700M to spend" in a pitch; that's aggregate
  across 2,769 orgs, not a number any single customer has.

## Why this isn't "another mosque management app"

Existing tools (Masjidbox, MOHID, Masjidaa, ConnectMazjid, My-Masjid, Masjid Suite) already
cover prayer times, announcements, event listings, and donations — they're organizing tools.
None of them do the *work* of planning an event, staffing it from a real volunteer roster, or
drafting the communications. That's the differentiation to keep saying out loud: "existing
software helps admins organize information; Khidmah helps them execute the work."
